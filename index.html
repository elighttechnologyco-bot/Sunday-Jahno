About Me

The person behind the work

Engineer, mathematician, computer scientist, teacher and company leader — here is where I come from and what I bring to the table.

Who I am

I am Jahno Udonde, an Engineer, Mathematician and Computer Scientist. I am a citizen of Nigeria, from Ikono Local Government Area of Akwa Ibom State, and I was born and raised in Calabar, Cross River State, on Saturday, 22 September 2001 at 1:30 pm.

I grew up curious about how things work — taking apart radios before I understood what a resistor was, and writing my first lines of code long before I knew the word “algorithm”. That curiosity turned into a profession that now spans four areas: software, engineering, teaching and business.

Today I build full-stack web applications, install and maintain electrical and solar power systems, repair consumer electronics down to component level, teach mathematics and technology to students at secondary, polytechnic and university level, and lead five companies in different capacities.

Faith & values

I was raised in a Witness family, and in 2019 I made the truth my own after careful personal research. I came to see that this is the only true organization approved by Jehovah.

I dedicated my life to Jehovah and symbolized that dedication in water baptism on Saturday, 21 September 2019, at the “Love Never Fails” Convention of Jehovah's Witnesses held in Akampa LGA, Cross River State.

That decision shapes how I work. Honesty in pricing. Care with other people's property. Patience with students who are struggling. And a genuine desire to leave every client, student and colleague better off than I met them.

Software & Digital

Building, securing and maintaining the digital side of your business — from a single page that loads fast to a full application your team relies on every day.

Web Design & Development

A website is often the first impression a customer has of your business. I build sites that load quickly, look right on every screen size, and are easy for you to update. Whether you need a simple landing page to advertise a service, a company site with several pages, or a full web application with logins, payments and a database, the process is the same: I listen to what you need, plan it properly, build it, and test it on real phones and computers before handing it over. You also get basic training on how to manage the content yourself.

Mobile App Development

Mobile applications for Android and iOS, built around how your users actually behave — not around what is easiest to code. I design the screens, build the logic, connect it to your data, and test it on real devices. This is useful for businesses that need to take orders, track deliveries, manage records, run a loyalty scheme, or simply stay in touch with customers. I can build a cross-platform app that works on both systems, which usually saves time and money compared to building twice.

Game Development

Games are not only for entertainment. I build 2D and 3D games that teach, train and advertise — for schools, training centres, businesses and individuals. The work covers concept and mechanics, the visuals, the scoring and levels, and a finished build you can install and play. Whether it is a simple educational game for children or a more complex simulation, the same care goes into making it run smoothly and feel fair to play.

Cybersecurity

Most people discover their systems are weak only after something has gone wrong. My work is to find those weaknesses first. I assess your website, network or application, identify where it can be attacked, and then help you fix it. This includes checking passwords and access controls, testing whether data can leak, reviewing how your staff use the system, and hardening the servers. You get a clear written report in plain English — what was found, how serious it is, and exactly what to do about it — not a page of jargon you cannot act on.

Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing

An ethical hacker is hired to break into your system — with your written permission — so that a real attacker cannot. I test your website, application or network the way an intruder would, but within an agreed scope and without causing damage. Then I document everything: how the weakness was found, what someone could do with it, and how to close it. This is valuable before launching a new product, after a security scare, or as a routine check for any business that holds customer data.

Engineering & Technical

Power, wiring, repairs and automation — designed properly, installed to standard, tested, and left safe. Every job ends with the same question: would I be comfortable if this were my own house?

Electrical Installation — Complete & Half Conduit

Wiring for new buildings, renovations and extensions — residential, commercial and industrial. In a complete conduit job, all cables run inside pipes buried in the wall, which protects them and makes future changes easier. In a half conduit job, part of the wiring is surface-mounted and part is concealed, which suits buildings that are already finished. Either way, I size the cables correctly for the load, install proper earthing and protection, label the distribution board clearly, and test everything before handing over. Poorly sized wiring is the cause of most electrical fires, so this is not a job to guess at.

Industrial Wiring

Factories, workshops, mills and production facilities need more than domestic wiring. I handle motor control panels, distribution boards, three-phase installations, machine wiring, contactors, overload protection and control circuits. The work is done to standard, with every circuit clearly identified, so that when a fault happens — and eventually one will — your technician can find it quickly instead of tracing cables for hours. I can also design the layout for a new production line from scratch.

Repair & Maintenance of Machines & Equipment

Diagnosis, repair and preventive maintenance of electric motors, generators, control panels, inverters, pumps and electronic equipment. I also rewind AC and DC electric motors to their original specification. Beyond fixing what is broken, I offer scheduled servicing — checking insulation, tightening terminals, cleaning cooling paths, and replacing worn parts before they fail. This matters because a machine that is serviced regularly costs far less over its life than one that is repaired only after it stops working.

Solar & Inverter Installation

Design and installation of solar panels, inverters, charge controllers and battery banks. The most important step happens before anything is bought: I audit your actual load — every light, fridge, freezer, pump, TV and air conditioner — and calculate what size of system you truly need. This is why many installations disappoint: they were sized from a guess. Done correctly, a solar system gives you steady power through outages, reduces your generator fuel cost, and lasts for years with minimal maintenance.

Repairs — Plasma & Smart TVs, Laptops, iPhones, Android Phones

Component-level troubleshooting and repair of plasma TVs, smart TVs, laptops running different operating systems, iPhones and Android phones. I diagnose the fault properly before quoting — no replacing of parts on a guess. Common jobs include power supply faults, no-display issues, broken charging ports, water damage, software problems, failed hard drives and screen replacements. If a device is genuinely not worth repairing, I will say so honestly and explain why, rather than take your money for something that will fail again next month.

Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing (MEP) Design

Coordinated design of the mechanical, electrical and plumbing services in a building — so the air conditioning, water supply, drainage, power and lighting all fit together from the drawing stage, instead of being forced to fit on site. Good MEP design prevents the situation where a pipe runs exactly where a cable tray needs to go, or a beam sits where a duct should pass. It saves money during construction and prevents expensive corrections later. I produce the drawings and the specifications your contractor will work from.

Automation

PLC and microcontroller-based automation for workshops, water systems, security, production lines and buildings. This includes sensors, motor control, level and flow monitoring, automatic switching, alarm systems and process control. Automation reduces the need for someone to stand and watch a machine or manually start a pump — the system does it, reliably, and tells you when something is wrong. It also improves consistency, because a controller does not get tired or distracted.

Academy & Tutoring

One-on-one and small-group teaching that builds real understanding, not memorisation. My goal is that a student can solve a problem they have never seen before — which is exactly what examinations and working life require.

Further Mathematics & General Mathematics

From basic algebra through to calculus, vectors, mechanics, statistics and complex numbers. I teach the reasoning behind each method, not just the steps — so a student understands why the formula works and can recognise when to use it. This matters because examination questions rarely look exactly like the examples in the textbook. For students preparing for WAEC, NECO, NABTE, GCE, JAMB, polytechnic or university examinations, I start by finding the gap. Most students who "hate maths" are not weak — they missed something two or three years ago that nobody went back to fill. Once that is fixed, progress is usually fast.

Physics Electronics & Chemistry

Clear explanations, worked examples and plenty of practice. In physics I cover mechanics, electricity, electronics, waves and modern physics. In chemistry, physical, organic and inorganic topics, with attention to the calculations that trip students up. I use real examples wherever possible — the physics of a solar panel or the chemistry of a battery means more to a student than an abstract diagram, and it is remembered longer.

Technical Drawings

Orthographic projection, isometric drawing, sectional views, dimensioning, tolerances and engineering conventions — taught the way technicians and engineers actually use them on site, not only the way they appear in an examination. A student who can read a drawing correctly will work faster and make fewer mistakes than one who cannot, and this is a skill that carries directly into engineering practice.

Computer Science & Basic Electricity

Programming fundamentals, computer architecture, logic, algorithms and basic electrical theory — voltage, current, resistance, power, series and parallel circuits, and the use of test instruments. Ideal for beginners starting a computing course, technicians upgrading their skills, and students preparing for technical examinations. I combine both subjects because in modern work they overlap: almost every electrical job now involves some electronics, and most automation involves both.

Sales & Supplies

Genuine products, fair prices, and advice you can trust. I would rather tell you that a cheaper option is fine than sell you something you do not need.

Building Materials

Supply of quality building materials for construction and renovation projects — sourced from trusted suppliers and delivered where you need them. Because I also work on site as a contractor, I know which materials hold up in practice and which ones cause problems six months later. That means the advice that comes with the supply is based on real installation experience, not just a price list.

Electronics & Gadgets

iPhones, Android phones and laptops running different operating systems — genuine devices at fair prices, with honest advice on what suits your budget and needs. Not everyone needs the most expensive phone, and not everyone should buy the cheapest laptop. I will ask what you actually do with it and recommend accordingly. Devices can also be set up and configured before delivery, so you are ready to use it immediately.

Electrical & Electronic Equipment, Spare Parts, Tools & Components

Components, tools and spare parts for electrical and electronic work — cables, breakers, contactors, relays, sensors, resistors, capacitors, transistors, connectors, test instruments, soldering equipment and more. If you are a technician mid-job and need the right part, I can help you find it the first time, so your work does not stall while you search the market. Bulk supply for contractors and workshops is also available.

General Contracting

One accountable person for the whole job. You deal with me, get one clear quotation, and know exactly who to call if anything needs attention afterwards.

End-to-End Project Delivery

I take responsibility for the whole project — planning, sourcing, execution, supervision and handover. You do not have to coordinate five different tradesmen or chase three suppliers. You get one quotation that covers the work, one point of contact throughout, a clear timeline, and a single person who remains accountable after completion. For renovation, installation, fit-out or construction work, this is often the difference between a project that finishes and one that drags on for months.

Sub-Contracting & Team Management

For larger jobs, I assemble and manage a vetted team of electricians, technicians, plumbers and tradesmen. I supervise the work personally, check it against the specification at each stage, and remain accountable for the final result. If you are a main contractor looking for a reliable sub-contractor for the electrical, electronic or MEP portions of a project, this is where I can help — with proper documentation and clear communication throughout.

How I Work

Four steps, every time

Whether it is a website or a wiring installation, the process is the same — because it is what prevents surprises.

Step 1

Listen & Assess

You explain what you need. I ask questions, look at the site or the system, and identify what is actually required — which is sometimes different from the first idea.

Step 2

Plan & Quote

You receive a clear quotation: what will be done, what materials will be used, how long it will take, and what it will cost. No hidden additions later.

Step 3

Execute & Test

The work is carried out to standard, with you kept informed of progress. At the end, everything is tested and demonstrated — not just handed over and left.

Step 4

Support

If something needs attention afterwards, you know exactly who to call. I stand behind my work and answer questions long after the job is finished.
